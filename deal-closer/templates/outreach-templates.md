# Outreach Templates

> Every outreach must reference a specific pain point or business outcome. Never "just checking in."

## Template 1: Cold LinkedIn (personalized)

**Subject/connection note:**
Hi [FIRST_NAME], I noticed [SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION: their hiring surge / recent funding / industry challenge / content they posted]. We helped [SIMILAR_COMPANY] solve [SPECIFIC_PAIN] and saw [QUANTIFIED_OUTCOME]. Worth a 15-min chat?

**Follow-up (Day 3):**
[FIRST_NAME] — circling back. Curious: what's your current approach to [PAIN_AREA]? We've seen teams like [PEER_COMPANY] spending [X HOURS/WEEK] on this. Happy to share what worked for them.

**Follow-up (Day 7):**
Not sure if this is a priority right now, but wanted to share [SPECIFIC_VALUE: case study / benchmark / insight]. If the timing is better later, I'm here.

**Follow-up (Day 14):**
Last touch from me. If [PAIN] isn't a pressing issue, no worries. If it is, I'm a reply away.

## Template 2: Cold Email (short, value-first)

**Subject:** [PAIN_POINT] at [COMPANY]?

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

[ONE_SENTENCE: reference their specific situation — "Saw [COMPANY] is [growing fast / launching X / facing Y challenge]"]

We helped [SIMILAR_COMPANY] [QUANTIFIED_OUTCOME: reduce X by Y% / save $Z / close W deals faster].

Worth 15 minutes to see if we can do the same for [COMPANY]?

[TITLE]
[COMPANY]
[PHONE]

## Template 3: Warm Intro Request

**To mutual connection:**
Hi [MUTUAL_CONNECTION], I'm reaching out to [TARGET_NAME] at [COMPANY] about [PAIN_AREA]. I noticed you're connected — any chance you'd be willing to make an intro? I'd keep it brief and value-first. Happy to draft the intro message for you.

## Template 4: Follow-Up After Meeting

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

Great chatting today. As promised:
- [DELIVERABLE_1]
- [DELIVERABLE_2]

Next step: [CLEAR_ACTION — "I'll send the proposal by Thursday" / "You'll connect me with [EB]"]

Let me know if anything changed on your end.

## Cadence Rules
| Touch | Timing | Channel | Purpose |
|-------|--------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | LinkedIn or Email | First value touch |
| 2 | Day 3 | LinkedIn | Follow-up with additional value |
| 3 | Day 7 | Email | Share case study / insight |
| 4 | Day 14 | LinkedIn | Last touch, leave door open |
| 5 | Day 30 | Email | Nurture: "still relevant?" |

After 5 touches, no response → move to nurture (quarterly check-in).

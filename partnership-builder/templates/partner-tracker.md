## Stages
1. **Research** — Identified, initial intel gathered
2. **Qualification** — Scorecard completed, score >=28
3. **Outreach** — First contact made
4. **Negotiation** — Terms discussed
5. **Closed** — Agreement signed
6. **Operating** — Active partnership, measuring results
7. **Review** — Quarterly assessment, skillify learnings

## Quarterly review checklist
- [ ] All active partnerships have metrics tracked
- [ ] Partner satisfaction surveyed (>=4/5 target)
- [ ] Revenue attribution updated
- [ ] New partner candidates identified
- [ ] Learnings from closed/failed partnerships documented
